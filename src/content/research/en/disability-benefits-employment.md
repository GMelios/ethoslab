---
title: The Employment Effects of Disability Benefits Without Work Restrictions
lang: en
type: working-paper
date: 2025-08-04
authors:
  - Bouke Klein Teeselink
  - George Melios
venue: SSRN working paper
url: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5378340
summary: Uses a 2013 reform of the UK's Personal Independence Payment, which carries no earnings test, to separate income effects from work disincentives. Benefit receipt shifts substantially, but people who gain or lose benefits through condition-specific eligibility changes do not change how much they work.
sectors:
  - labour
  - health
methods:
  - Difference-in-differences
  - Matched panel data
highlights:
  - Benefit receipt changed by 1.7 to 5.9 percentage points across identification strategies.
  - For people who gained or lost benefits through condition-specific eligibility changes, employment effects are clear nulls.
  - Regions assigned to the stricter assessor show an employment differential of about three percentage points, read as reduced-form evidence because the first stage is weak.
