---
title: The Employment Effects of Disability Benefits Without Work Restrictions
lang: en
type: working-paper
date: 2025-08-04
authors:
  - Bouke Klein Teeselink
  - George Melios
venue: SSRN working paper
url: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5378340
summary: Uses a 2013 reform of the UK's Personal Independence Payment, which carries no earnings test, to separate income effects from work disincentives. Benefit receipt shifts substantially, but employment effects are close to zero outside stricter assessment regions.
sectors:
  - labour
  - health
methods:
  - Difference-in-differences
  - Matched panel data
highlights:
  - Benefit receipt changed by 1.8 to 5.9 percentage points across identification strategies.
  - Positive employment effects appear only in regions with stricter assessment providers.
  - For people who gained or lost benefits through condition-specific eligibility changes, employment effects are close to zero.
