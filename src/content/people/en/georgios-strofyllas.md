Economist/ Behavioural Scientist

George Strofyllas is an Economist/Researcher at Ethos Lab, where he designs and implements behavioral experiments to inform impactful and sustainable public policy. With a strong foundation in behavioral economics, experimental methods, and data analytics, he leverages both academic and industry experience to uncover the psychological and contectual drivers of decision-making.

George holds an MSc in Economics and Business from Erasmus University Rotterdam, where his research focused on human behavior under risk and uncertainty. Prior to joining Ethos Lab, he worked as an Analytics Expert at Circana, leading data-driven business analysis and process optimization for corporate clients. Proficient in R, SQL, Stata, and advanced visualization tools, George combines technical fluency with a deep understanding of economic behavior to deliver policy-relevant insights and drive measurable impact.
