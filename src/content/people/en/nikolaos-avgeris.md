Nikos Avgeris is a Senior Economist at Ethos Lab. He has more than a decade consultancy experience for governments and international organizations (World Bank, ILO, OECD) on technical reform projects and policy research. He holds an MSc from the University of Manchester in Development Economics and Policy. His research area of focus is centered around labour economics, machine learning econometrics, impact evaluations and M&E. He focuses on employing data driven solutions to conduct research & provide policy insights, evaluation and recommendations for strategic decision making in a wide area of economic and business topics. Nikos is  seasoned at employing advanced econometric, machine learning techniques and creating complex and interactive visualizations, as well as communicating insights to non-data savvy audiences.
