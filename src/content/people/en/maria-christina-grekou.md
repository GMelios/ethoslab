Executive Assistant

Maria Christina Grekou has a background in political science and international negotiations, and brings strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and experience to complex, deadline-driven environments. She is an Executive Assistant and Project Manager at EthosLab, where she coordinates multidisciplinary projects and ensures the seamless execution of research and consulting activities. She manages administrative and organizational processes, supports cross-team communication, and streamlines workflows that enable researchers and analysts to focus on high-impact deliverables. Her role integrates project oversight with executive support, ensuring efficiency, accuracy, and effective collaboration across stakeholders.
