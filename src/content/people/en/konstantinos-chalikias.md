Junior Economist

Konstantinos Chalikias is a Data Analyst at Ethos Lab, where he supports research and policy projects through data-driven insights and applied statistical analysis. Holding a BSc in statistics from Athens University of Economics and Business, Konstantinos has a strong foundation in econometrics, data analysis and applied machine learning.

He is proficient in R, Python and SQL and is passionate about turning complex data into clear insights and developing analytical tools that capture behavioral patterns and support decision-making.
