What will ESG mean for small and medium-sized enterprises in the next few years? The final meeting of ESG LAB, on 14 September 2026 in Piraeus and online, brought together the main results, tools and lessons of the project: ESG learning resources, practical tools and the experience of mentoring SMEs.

Part of the meeting was given to invited speakers, who shared perspectives on the future of ESG, competitiveness, finance, supply chains and the changing expectations on Blue Economy SMEs.

ESG LAB helps SMEs in the Blue Economy adopt ESG practices through training, a learning platform and a Community of Practice. It is co-funded by the Erasmus+ programme.
