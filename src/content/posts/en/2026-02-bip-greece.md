Behavioural Insights for Policy (BiP) works to expand the reach of behavioural public policy beyond the places where it is already established. After a first edition in Moldova, BiP held its first event in Greece on 26 and 27 February 2026 at the ETERON Institute in Athens, co-organised by Ethos Lab and ETERON.

The two-day workshop combined academic and policy presentations, panel discussions and keynote lectures from scholars and practitioners across academia, government and industry. The keynote speakers were Professor Ralph Hertwig, Director of the Center for Adaptive Rationality at the Max Planck Institute for Human Development, and Eva Koromilas, Project Lead at the OECD Observatory of Public Sector Innovation.
