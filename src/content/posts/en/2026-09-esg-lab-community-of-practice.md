On 10 September 2026, the National Community of Practice of the ESG LAB project met online. The meeting introduced the project and its partnership, set out the role of the national and transnational Communities of Practice, and opened an interactive discussion on the challenges, support needs and good practices of putting ESG into practice.

It closed with a presentation of the training materials, digital resources and tools available to participating organisations, and agreed next steps to keep the community active.
