On 5 June 2026, as part of the City of Piraeus Sea Days, ESG LAB held the event "SMEs, ESG and the Blue Economy" at the Municipal Gallery of Piraeus. Representatives of local government, academics, sustainability and ESG specialists, Blue Economy executives and business advisers took part.

The Deputy Mayor for Planning and Sustainable Development of Piraeus, Dimitris Karydis, opened the event and stressed the importance of ESG and the Blue Economy for the city's SMEs. Two round tables followed: one on the Blue Economy, ESG and new opportunities, and one in which Blue Economy representatives and businesses discussed the ESG Community of Practice. The event was moderated by Dr Nikolaos Melios, Co-Founder of Ethos Lab.

ESG LAB is co-funded by the Erasmus+ programme.
